About Us

Once, there was a loving father who wanted to provide for his family and was tempted to leave his country of birth. The very reason was, that he was about to start from scratch, after deciding to leave the decade-long employment of his comfort zone. Pet grooming was the stepping stone until he was able to get back on his own feet, to practice his profession again, as a veterinarian, but then at those times, were his own. Pet grooming wasn't considered a menial job but a purpose that only a few will get. Lyne Pet Grooming Services, formerly known as PetHub, was established by our loving late Padre de Pamilya, Dr. Hilbart S. Pizarro aka Doc Bong. We changed the business's name for legal purposes because it was named after our late father, husband, and brother, who is now deceased. He started, supported by his wife, with only a DIY wooden table and a store-bought pair of scissors in 2016, full of hope, giving our effort to give lovely pets a refreshing groom. Until innovation came, clippers and blades joined the service. There's no guarantee what the future may hold, but pet grooming puts food on the table. We made social media accounts for clients who are looking for pet grooming services online, using these apps as their search engine. Thank you for making it this far and choosing us, lovely pet owners. God bless you all❤️.
